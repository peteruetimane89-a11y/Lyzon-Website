Health, Safety and Environmental Policy

We are committed to safe working conditions, active employee participation, and responsible environmental performance aligned with ISO 45001:2018.

Request a Quote Back to Home

Standard

ISO 45001:2018

Occupational Health and Safety Management System framework for risk-based prevention and continual improvement.

Principle

Prevention First

Eliminate hazards, reduce risk, and build safe practices into every task and every site.

Lyzon Engineering & Services, Lda. specializes in supplying electrical, instrumentation, automation, and HVAC equipment, as well as installation and maintenance services for low- and medium-voltage electrical systems, general mechanics, solar photovoltaic systems, and related engineering.

Safety and environmental protection are of paramount importance to the operations of the company. Lyzon Engineering & Services, Lda. is committed to implementing an Occupational Health and Safety Management System in accordance with ISO 45001:2018.

What We Believe

  • All accidents can be prevented and all work can be done safely.

  • Managers are responsible for the health and safety of those they control within the work environment and for encouraging the sustainable use of renewable resources.

  • Everyone has a duty to work safely and encourage others to do the same.

  • It is possible to measure and manage the environmental impact of our activities.

Our Policy

  • Provide safe and healthy working conditions by eliminating hazards and reducing risks.

  • Establish safe work practices and procedures.

  • Train employees on occupational safety.

  • Eliminate unsafe conditions and unsafe acts in the workplace.

  • Ensure employee consultation and participation.

  • Create methodologies that ensure our full commitment to the continuous improvement of the Occupational Safety and Health and Environmental Management System.

  • Actively promote the establishment of robust and viable codes of practice and bylaws for safety, environmental protection, and pollution prevention.

Our Commitment

  • Ensure the safety of our employees and everyone who may be affected by our activities.

  • Empower and motivate all employees to consider environmental aspects as an integral and vital element of their daily activities.

  • Comply with legislation applicable to Occupational Health, Safety, and Environmental standards.
